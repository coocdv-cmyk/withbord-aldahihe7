# episode-04-en — on-screen script (silent)

A text-only explainer in English: how to read a book in a week. There is no
voice track at all — the writing on the board carries the whole argument, and
the only sound is the pen and the paper.

---

1.  Seven days, one book, a plan you can keep.
2.  Day one: read the first hour out loud. Your voice catches what your eyes skim.
3.  Day two: write three questions. A question you cannot answer is worth keeping.
4.  Day three: reread only the pages you marked.
5.  Day four: argue with the author. Write the disagreement in one sentence.
6.  Day five: teach one idea to someone. If you cannot, you have not read it.
7.  Day six: write the whole book in one sentence.
8.  Day seven: keep the book or leave it. Both answers are honest.
